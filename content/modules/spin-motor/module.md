A loop of wire, one battery and a magnet. Put them together the right way and the loop spins by itself. In two hours you find out why a current and a magnet push on each other, build your own motor, and make it go faster.

1. **The spinning coil** (15 min). Watch the guide's motor spin, learn the safety rules, and predict what makes it turn.
2. **Current makes magnets** (35 min). A battery-and-magnet warm-up motor, a compass that swings near a wire, and an electromagnet made from a nail.
3. **Build the motor** (45 min). Wind a coil, scrape it the clever way, and get it spinning.
4. **Faster, and why** (25 min). Speed it up, turn a motor into a generator, and explain your motor to the guide.

By the end you'll understand that a current makes a magnetic field, that a magnet pushes on a current, and how a motor uses that push to keep turning. Every fan, water pump and bajaj starter motor works the same way.
