## The rules first

Before you go in, your guide reads out the rules. Copy them into your notebook:

1. Never point a pinhole camera at the Sun. Never look at the Sun, ever.
2. Pins stay in their box or stuck in card.
3. Scissors only. The guide does any cutting with a blade.
4. With a hood over your head, stand still. Never walk with it on.

## Watch

Your guide has covered the windows of a room so it is completely dark, apart from one small hole in the cover. Go in, close the door and wait. Your eyes need two or three minutes to get used to the dark.

Look at the wall opposite the hole. Look carefully at:

- what you can see, and whether it is moving,
- which way up it is,
- what happens when someone outside walks past, or a car or bajaj goes by,
- what happens when the guide covers half the hole, then makes it bigger.

## Predict

On a page titled *The picture from a hole*, write:

1. **Where does the picture come from?** Write your best guess, then a second one.
2. **Why is it upside down?**
3. **What would happen if the hole were much bigger?** Write your guess. You'll test it in step 3.

## Questions

As a team, list every question you have. For example:

- Why do we need a dark room?
- Is left and right swapped too?
- Why is the picture so dim?
- Would the picture be bigger if the wall were further away?

Here is a clue: **light travels in straight lines.** In the next step you'll prove it.
