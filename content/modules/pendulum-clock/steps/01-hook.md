## Watch

Your guide hangs a padlock on a long string and pulls it a little to one side. A phone on the desk clicks once every second. The guide lets go.

Look carefully at:

- where the padlock is each time the phone clicks,
- whether the beat stays the same as the swings get smaller,
- what happens when the guide swaps in a **heavier padlock** on the same string,
- what happens when the guide **shortens** the string to about a quarter of its length.

## The words you need

- **Full swing:** from one side, across, and back to where it started.
- **Period:** the time for one full swing, in seconds.
- **Tick:** one swing from one side to the other. Every full swing has two ticks.

The guide's first pendulum ticks once a second. So its period is **2 seconds**.

## Predict

On a page titled *The beat*, write your guess for each question. Don't worry about being right yet.

1. A **heavier** nut on the same string: faster, slower, or the same?
2. A **bigger** swing (pulled further back): faster, slower, or the same?
3. A **longer** string: faster, slower, or the same?
4. **Guess:** how long must the string be for a pendulum that ticks once a second? Each person writes a number in centimetres.

## Questions

As a team, list every question you have. For example: Why did the heavy padlock not swing faster? Does a swing at a playground follow the same rule? How did old clocks keep the swing going?

You'll answer the first three predictions yourself in the next step, with fair tests.
