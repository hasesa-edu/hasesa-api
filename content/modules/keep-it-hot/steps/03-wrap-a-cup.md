## Theory: a fair test

Read **Fair tests and averages**. You'll learn:

- the **independent** variable (what you change), the **dependent** variable (what you measure) and the **controlled** variables (what you keep the same),
- why you need a **control**: a cup with no insulation, to compare against,
- how to write a fair-test plan in five lines.

## Plan (5 minutes)

Write your plan:

1. **Question:** Does our insulation keep water hotter than a bare cup?
2. **I will change:** the wrapping (none on the control cup; our design on the other).
3. **I will measure:** the temperature of each cup every 5 minutes for 30 minutes.
4. **I will keep the same:** ___ (list at least four things).
5. **Control:** an identical cup with nothing on it, next to ours on the same tray.

Show the plan to your guide.

## Build (5 minutes)

Use your drawing from step 2. Choose materials to block as many ways of losing heat as you can: something for the sides, something for the bottom, and a lid with a small hole for the thermometer. Wrap the sides and bottom of your **empty** cup now, standing it on the tray, so you never have to lift a cup of hot water. Keep the lid ready beside it.

Ideas: layers of newspaper or cloth, cotton wool or straw packed around the cup and held with string or a rubber band, foil as the outside layer, shiny side out, a folded cardboard lid.

## Do: the cooling race (30 minutes)

1. The guide pours **200 mL** of hot water into both your cups, from the same flask, one straight after the other.
2. Put the lid on your insulated cup. Leave the control cup bare.
3. Stir each cup gently with its thermometer and read the start temperature. Both should be close to 60 °C. Write them down and start the timer.
4. Every **5 minutes**, read both temperatures. Push the thermometer through the hole in the lid, stir gently, wait until the reading stops changing, then read. Put the lid straight back.
5. Don't move the cups, and keep them out of the sun and away from doors and windows.

Record every reading in a table like this:

| Time (min) | Control (°C) | Insulated (°C) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | | |
| 5 | | |
| 10 | | |
| ... | | |
| 30 | | |

While you wait between readings, look at your control cup's column. Is it falling by the same amount every 5 minutes, or not?

## Record

At 30 minutes, work out how much each cup cooled: **start temperature minus final temperature**. Write one sentence comparing them. Then check your guess from step 1: how close were you?
