A hot drink cools down on its own, but a thermos can keep it hot for hours. In two hours you find out the four ways heat escapes, wrap a cup to block them, and prove with numbers and a graph how well your design works.

1. **Cold buna** (15 min). Compare a cup and a thermos, predict, and learn the hot-water rules.
2. **Where does the heat go?** (35 min). Quick experiments with spoons, lids, foil and black paper show conduction, convection, radiation and evaporation.
3. **Wrap a cup** (45 min). Design your insulation and run a fair test against a bare control cup, taking the temperature every 5 minutes.
4. **The cooling curve** (25 min). Graph your results, compare designs, and explain how a thermos works.

By the end you'll understand how heat moves, how to run a fair test with a control, and how to read a cooling curve.
