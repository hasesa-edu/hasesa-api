A steel ship floats, but a steel bolt sinks. In two hours you find out why, measure the push of water for yourself, and design a foil boat that carries as many coins as it can.

1. **Steel that floats** (15 min). Watch a bolt and a foil ball sink and a foil boat float. Predict and ask questions.
2. **Pushed-aside water** (35 min). Catch the water a floating object pushes aside and weigh it. Learn about density and upthrust.
3. **Design your boat** (45 min). Fold a boat from one sheet of foil, predict how many coins it can carry, then test it.
4. **The final** (25 min). The class competition, prediction against result, and why real ships are shaped the way they are.

By the end you'll understand density, upthrust, pressure in water and balanced forces, and you'll be able to predict how much a boat can carry before you put a single coin in it.
