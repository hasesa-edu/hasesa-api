How do you lift something much heavier than you can push? Watch any building site in Addis: a worker shifts a concrete block with an iron bar, or prises up a slab that two people couldn't lift. In two hours you find the rule behind every lever, use it to predict the exact force you need, and prove it with a scale.

1. **Two fingers and a plank** (15 min). Watch the smallest student lift a sack of sand, and learn the plank rules.
2. **Balance the ruler** (35 min). Balance coins on a ruler and discover the law of the lever.
3. **Lift the sack** (45 min). Build a plank lever, predict the pull you need, and measure it.
4. **Levers everywhere** (25 min). Find the levers in scissors, a wheelbarrow, a bottle opener and your own arm, and explain what a lever costs you.

By the end you'll understand moments, the law of the lever, mechanical advantage, the three classes of lever and why no lever ever gives you something for nothing.
